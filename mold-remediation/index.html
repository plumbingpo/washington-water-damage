Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Discolored or darkened spots near HVAC vents, across ceilings or on walls
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Persistent musty or earthy odors, especially in enclosed or humid areas
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.

A certified technician goes through the property to find mold both in plain view and out of sight, traces the moisture feeding it and, where useful, takes surface or air samples for a lab to analyze.

3

Following EPA guidelines, our crew removes moldy material with care and handles its disposal. A professional-grade antimicrobial solution is then applied to the structural surfaces that stay.

4

We address the underlying moisture source, complete any necessary drying and perform post-remediation verification to confirm the remediation succeeded before releasing containment.

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not leave damp laundry, bath mats or spilled water sitting for long
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Repair any leak inside 24 hours, since 24 to 48 hours of moisture is all mold requires to begin
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Keep gutters clean so rainwater does not seep in along the foundation
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
