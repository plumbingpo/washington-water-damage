Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.

Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
